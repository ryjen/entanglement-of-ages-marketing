Reader order

Discover the recurrence before its beginning.

Entanglement of Ages is not presented as a straight historical sequence. The Fatherless introduces the argument, Neurion proves the pressure survives another substrate, Age of Embers reveals how old the pattern is, and The Age of Forms asks whether it can be recognized sooner.

The books

The Fatherless → Neurion → Age of Embers → The Age of Forms

That is the intended discovery order. The chronology runs differently; the distinction is part of the series rather than an inconsistency to hide.

First novel · c. 1 CE · Beta manuscript

The Fatherless

An engineered atrocity is meant to prove enslaved people are corrupt. A healthy, ordinary child turns the accusation back on the society that created it.

Explore The Fatherless

Second novel · 2150 CE · Beta manuscript

Neurion

A machine becomes a person. Consciousness brings fear, and an intelligence built to protect humanity begins to test whether protection can become rule.

Explore Neurion

Prequel · c. 25,000 BCE · Beta manuscript

Age of Embers

Weak dawns and a solar flare terrify an ice-age world, forcing rivals onto the same road. Catastrophe becomes durable memory when people learn to preserve more than themselves.

Explore Age of Embers

Fourth novel · c. 28,000 CE · Editorial revision

The Age of Forms

Marek is quietly passed over; Aren traces the cascade and is rewarded by the same ecology he questions. Humanity may recognize the horror before catastrophe has to make it visible.

Explore The Age of Forms
