Private editorial participation

Beta editors and early readers.

Four novels are in beta. We plan to invite thoughtful readers and editors to help find what works, what drags, and what needs another look.

You don't need professional editing credentials. We welcome developmental editors, beta readers and subject-matter readers. Interest in one book or all four is welcome; the intended reader order is The Fatherless, Neurion, Age of Embers, then The Age of Forms.

How it works

  1. Registration will open here after consent and mailing-list verification.
  2. If there's a suitable reading round, we'll contact you with its scope, expected timing, and confidentiality requirements.
  3. Only approved invitees receive private reading material and instructions for submitting feedback.

Signing up is not a manuscript download, an editing appointment, or a promise of an invitation. Feedback from invited readers stays in private correspondence, not public GitHub issues.

Registration opening later

Beta editor registration is not open yet. Consent records, sender contact details, and delivery must be verified before email collection begins.

No email addresses are being collected on this page. Joining the future interest list will not grant manuscript access; approved editors receive separate private invitations.

For editorial questions, contact info@ryanjennin.gs.

Want to describe your editing background or a specific area of expertise? Use the public contributor-interest form, but do not post email addresses, private notes, or unpublished story details there. That form is optional and requires GitHub.
