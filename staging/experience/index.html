Across the ages

Follow what survives.

The story threads are not a reading prerequisite. They offer a spoiler-conscious view of recurring questions through different civilizations.

The Great Age

Comparable celestial turnings recur across immense stretches of time, without proving what they mean. The ages echo one another; humanity does not return unchanged.

Embers ↔ Fatherless · Neurion ↔ Forms

The Habirim

Witnesses carry uncertain observation through civilizations that are tempted to turn their interpretations into final authority. The institution changes; the need to preserve contradiction remains.

Observe → preserve → contradict → renew

Horror & miracle

The horror is when an imposed form claims to define a life. The ordinary miracle is that life, memory, personhood, or consciousness exceeds that claim.

Imposed meaning → life beyond the claim

Human evolution

Each age inherits a genuine gain, then confronts the temptation to make that gain an unquestionable source of authority. Recognizing the recurrence may itself be a form of progress.

Survival → dignity → plural consciousness → value beyond rank
